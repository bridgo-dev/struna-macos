# Struna for macOS

**Your music. Your folders. Your Mac.**

Struna is a local music player by [BridGO](https://bridgo.dev/). Play your own music from folders, organize favorites and playlists, and listen to internet radio.

**[Download Struna](https://strunaplayer.com/#download)** · [User guide](https://docs.strunaplayer.com/) · [Buy a license](https://bridgo.dev/struna/buy/) · [Website](https://strunaplayer.com/)

Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later, on Apple silicon or Intel. Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

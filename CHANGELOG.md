## 2.0.2

Build 68 · 2026-10-28

- A new Mac is confirmed with a short code sent to the purchase email when required.
- Paid licenses are checked about every 15 days; Struna keeps working offline for up to 30 days after the last successful check.
- Refunds and device deactivations take effect when the app next checks the license.

Requires macOS 13 or later. Universal build for Apple silicon and Intel. Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
